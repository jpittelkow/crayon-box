Coloring buttons a kid can work out

A prototype for ADR 0038. Tap the tools, sizes and colours as a kid would, and paint on the page. The yellow line shows what the narrator would say; the sounds here are simple beeps standing in for the real ones.

Kid hears: …

1. Names and sounds

Each button has its own sound. The narrator says its name for the first 3 picks per kid, then only the sound plays. Picks fire on touch-down.

2. The tool at the finger

A badge with the tool and colour follows a finger on the page. The picked tool is bigger, and the sizes grey out under the bucket.

3. Show-how

The first time a kid picks a tool, its badge does the job by itself in a corner while the narrator says one line. A touch stops it, and the picture stays unchanged.

5. Colour wheel

12 big segments of in-between colours, shown only where the screen has room beside the rail. Tap only, no dragging.
