## Session: sounds as files, part 1
**Done**
- Made the narrator's full set (A to Z, 0 to 9, four praise words) in Matilda and six police-siren takes, inside the
  sync container, with `tools/sounds/generate-narrator.mjs`.
- `tools/sounds/prepare.mjs` (needs ffmpeg) trims, levels and writes `assets/sounds/` and `sounds.json` from
  `tools/sounds/picks.json`. Touch sounds are WAV (no MP3 start delay); the voice is MP3. 45 files, about 0.6 MB.
- The shell plays the files (`src/shell/sound.ts`, `soundFiles.ts`), decoded ahead of the first touch. "Nothing" is
  the tap, quieter and lower. There is a new `page` sound when a coloring page opens.
- Letters: Matilda says the letters (small letters use their capital's clip) and a finished letter adds a random
  praise word. A parent's recording still wins. In the parent area, "Windows voice" became "The app's voice"; the
  stored value stays `windows` for settings that already exist.
- Tests: the sound files' lengths, coverage and credits (unit), and a narrator-then-praise check (end-to-end). The
  sounds test now counts buffer playback instead of tones.

**Learned**
- BytePlus's general AI terms give the user ownership of output; only its Playground terms bar publishing it.
- Playwright's own port (5391) keeps it off other dev servers; worth checking it is free when another worktree runs.
