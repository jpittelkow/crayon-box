## Session: playtest fixes, part 1 (Driving loads at once, back a page, Back to the picker)
**Done**
- The picker shows at once, and draws its pictures one at a time after it (vehicles on the open page first, then
  the maps, then the rest), so a tap is taken while they draw. Each picture is kept in memory for the session and,

  graphics card: 60 frames a second on both maps, so it never steps down there; the laptop is still unmeasured.

## Session: a plan for a very large map (highway and building site)
**Done**
- The owner asked for a plan for a very large driving map, "like a highway and construction site", made with the
  recipes. Written as [highway-map-plan.md](../highway-map-plan.md): the size in numbers, what breaks today, two
  layouts ([A, a ring round a lake](../design/highway-layout-a.svg), recommended;
  [B, a figure of eight with an overpass](../design/highway-layout-b.svg)), every piece sorted by the asset
  catalog with its recipe, a draft recipe with patterns and anti-recipes for a map this size, nine phases, risks,
  and ten choices for the owner (D1 to D10).
- Linked from the to-do list (sections 4 and 6) and the maps recipe. Nothing built, nothing committed. No ADR
  yet: it follows the owner's choices.

**Learned** (measured on `main` with a throwaway test)
- One simulation step of the island is 1.24 ms; without toys 0.03 ms. The cost is every vehicle checking every
  toy. The building site stretched four times across (5,440 toys, 40 traffic) took 105 ms a step: a big map
  can't run until vehicles only check what is near them.
- The island is 3.7 ha with 0.70 km of road; the building site 5.1 ha with 0.80 km.
- ADR 0035's "every place within 25 m of a road" is not what the maps are, and nothing tests it: about a quarter
  of each map's reachable land is further than that (farthest 46 m and 45 m).
- Stretching a map leaves an empty middle (69 % of the land over 40 m from a road): a big map needs more roads
  and water, not a bigger scale.
- A throwaway test's `console.log` did not show in vitest's output here; it wrote its numbers to a file instead.

**Decided** (owner)
- Layout B, the figure of eight with an overpass (layout A was recommended and stays the fallback). The plan was
  reworked around it: roads on two levels (phase 7) come before the map and are proven on a small test map
  first; toll gates, the gravel chute and the pipe yard were added to layout B's picture.

- The recommendation on every other choice but when to start: about 1,080 m across; no highway rules for the
  kid; the same top speed; no place more than 45 m from a road, tested; four start spots in turn; the first set
  of pieces; a road roller and a big lorry later. Drafted as
  [ADR 0040](../adr/0040-driving-very-large-map-highway-and-building-site.md) (Proposed).

**Learned**
- ADR numbers collide across branches: 0038 was taken on `main` (coloring buttons) and again on a branch
  (writing words) the same day, so this one is 0040 and leaves 0039 free. Local `main` was three commits behind
  `origin/main` while this was written.
- The island's farthest point from a road measured 46 m, a metre over the new 45 m rule: fix it or write its
  allowance when the test is added.
