## Session: driving to a spot, and traffic that keeps the kid company
**Done**
- Read ADR 0021 next to `src/games/racing/sim/` with the owner. Three gaps: steering came from how far across the
  screen the finger was (a held finger circled forever), traffic copied the kid's speed but never caught up or
  left, and a bump with traffic cost the kid's car a lot of speed.
- Built [ADR 0031](../adr/0031-driving-to-a-spot-and-traffic-company.md) (Proposed) on branch
  `driving/steer-and-traffic`: a touch picks a spot on the ground (`groundPoint` in `sim/view.ts`); the car drives
  there and stops, and gives up a spot it can't reach. One traffic car at a time catches up, rides alongside for

  steering really used; the tyre squeal reads it.
- Tests: 263 unit tests and the 4 driving browser tests pass. Checked by eye in screenshots: the car turning to
  a touched spot, and the monster truck alongside in the flood.
