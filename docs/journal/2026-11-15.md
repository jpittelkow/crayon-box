## Session: playtest fixes, part 1 (Driving loads at once, back a page, Back to the picker)
**Done**
- The picker shows at once, and draws its pictures one at a time after it (vehicles on the open page first, then
  the maps, then the rest), so a tap is taken while they draw. Each picture is kept in memory for the session and,
  in a release build, on the device under `local/pictures/<version>/` (`look/pictureCache.ts`), so the picker
  opens with its pictures from then on. Measured in the test browser: 6 s frozen before, about 1 s now.
- The map pictures' throwaway renderers now give their drawing context back at once (`dispose(true)`); before,
  each visit to the picker left two behind until they were collected, and a browser keeps only about 16.
- A "back a page" tile on the vehicle picker after the first page (`pickerPages.ts`, `icons.less`); pages no
  longer wrap round.
- Back in the game returns to the picker, whose Back goes home.
- The game turns its picture down on a computer that can't keep up (`detail.ts`): if frames average slower than
  22 ms (about 45 a second) it draws 20 % fewer pixels, step by step, down to half. Checked on the dev machine's
  graphics card: 60 frames a second on both maps, so it never steps down there; the laptop is still unmeasured.
- Tests: 332 unit tests (new: picker pages, picture cache, turning down) and all 95 browser tests pass.

**Not done**

- Coloring: button hints and a colour wheel need a design first.
- AI pages on the laptop: needs a closer look there (which buttons, typing in the idea box).

## Session: the animal pages
**Done**
- The owner asked for the animal coloring pages, "dinos and other animals". 22 pages made with GPT-5 Image Mini
  from the idea prompt plus "a cute, friendly cartoon animal" (ADR 0022, update of 2026-11-15):
  - **Dinosaurs**, a new ninth theme with its own icon: T. rex, long-neck, triceratops, stegosaurus, pterodactyl,
    baby dinosaur in an egg.
  - **Wild animals:** lion, elephant, giraffe, monkey, hippo, bear.
  - **Farm and pets:** cat, puppy, cow, pig, duck, bunny.
  - **Under the sea:** octopus, sea turtle, whale and crab, next to the fish.
- Two takes of each, picked by eye; the elephant, sea turtle and crab were made again with a prompt asking for a
  white margin, because their ears, flippers and claws ran off the picture. About $3.40 of OpenRouter calls.
- `tools/clean-pages.mjs`: the clean-up for a whole folder, in a real browser, with a report. It trims the faint
  frame these images have at the very edge (`--trim`), and where a tail or claw is cut by the edge it joins the
  two lines along that edge so the shape stays closed (`--join`).
- Theme order is now Race cars, Farm and pets, Wild animals, Dinosaurs, Under the sea, Things that go, Yummy food,
  (Outside), Shapes.
- Tests: all 334 unit tests and the page check on every page pass; colored the T. rex in the app.

**Learned**
- GPT-5 Image Mini through OpenRouter takes 40 to 70 s a picture and slows down with more than about six calls at
  once; three runs side by side were the quickest.
- Its pictures are square with a transparent or white background; lines that reach the edge leave a shape open
  once the page margin is added, so the margin has to be asked for in the prompt or closed afterwards.

**Next**

- A kid playtest of the animal pages: are the small areas (paws, toes) fine for little fingers?
