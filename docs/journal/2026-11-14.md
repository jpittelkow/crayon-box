## Session: three.js or Babylon.js, tested
**Decided** (by the owner)

**Done**
- `spikes/engine-bakeoff/`: a small island (ring road, trees, traffic that paces the car, props to knock,

**Learned** (dev machine; draw work only, page hidden, frames run by hand)
- The two pictures match: 0.013% of pixels differ.
- Download for this scene, compressed: three.js 131 kB, Babylon.js 278 kB.
- Batched drawing costs the same in both (about 0.1 ms a frame with 2,400 props). With one object per prop,
  Babylon.js took about nine times longer (4.3 ms against 0.5 ms for 1,200 objects).
- A first timing run was wrong: Babylon.js was still compiling shaders and drawing nothing. Re-measured.
- Checked in the pane: keys and touch drive, lifting stops the car, the car never passed 58.5 m from the centre
  of a 60 m island and covered 996 m in a simulated minute of mixed steering, and no prop ended up broken.
- Not checked: frame rate (the pane was hidden), sound by ear, a real touch screen, built-in graphics.
